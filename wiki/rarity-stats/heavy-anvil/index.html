Heavy Anvil

Reforge, upgrade and ascend — what each operation costs and what can go wrong.

Craft rrs:heavy_anvil and interact with it while holding a Rarity & Stats item. Costs are experience levels plus the item’s own material as a catalyst. In Creative mode every operation is free.

Reforge

Re-rolls the item’s passives from three locked options. A further button regenerates the options.

Regenerating the option set costs the reroll price scaled by the tier multiplier and by 0.5 per reroll already done, capped at 50 levels.

Upgrade risk

The first few upgrades of a tier are safe. After that each further upgrade carries a growing chance to regress — but never below what a previous tier had already secured.

A successful upgrade moves the passive 5%–12% of its range; a failure costs 2%–12%.

Ascend

Raises the item to the next tier once its passives are upgraded enough. Every passive rises with it, and eligible passives transform into their ascended forms.

Ascended is the top tier: it cannot be ascended further and its passives are locked at maximum. An addon that registers a higher tier through the API unlocks it.

Catalyst cost

Each operation also consumes the item’s own material. What that material is, and how much of it, is listed per tier on Materials. Combat cooldown after a hit is 30s — anvil operations are unaffected by it.
