Wiki / Rarity & Stats

Overview

What the addon does, and where to go next.

Rarity & Stats rolls a rarity and a set of passive abilities onto equipment the moment it is equipped. Rarity is weighted by the item’s material, and passives roll their own rarity and value out of a budget — so two items of the same tier can feel very different.

Rarity tiers

Seven tiers. Each one sets how many passives an item can carry and how many of those are guaranteed to be stat passives.

How an item is rolled

  • The item’s material picks the rarity — netherite reaches far higher tiers than wood, and each material can favour or exclude tiers outright.
  • The rolled rarity sets a budget; passives are drawn until the budget runs out, each costing according to its own rarity and value.
  • Stat passives give flat attribute bonuses and every item is guaranteed at least one.
  • Trigger passives fire in combat, and some transform into stronger forms as the item ascends.

The Heavy Anvil

Craft rrs:heavy_anvil and interact with it holding a Rarity & Stats item to reforge, upgrade individual passives, or ascend the item to the next tier. In Creative mode every operation is free.
