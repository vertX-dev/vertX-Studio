Rarities

The seven tiers, their roll odds, passive slots and budgets.

Rarity is rolled once, when an item is first equipped. The base weights below are then bent by the item’s material — see Materials.

Base chance is the raw weight share with no material applied. In practice no single item can roll every tier — most materials cap out well below Ascended.

Item budget

Each tier rolls a budget from a weighted set of options. The budget is what passives are bought with, so a lucky high roll means either one maxed passive or several modest ones.

Passive rarity weighting

When a passive is drawn, its own rarity is weighted by the item’s tier. Higher item tiers make high-rarity passives dramatically more likely — an Ascended item weights Ascended passives at 500.
