Crude tools

Every tool you can craft before you understand how a sharp edge works.

Crafting a tool before discovering Sharp Tools gives you a crude one instead: it looks like the tool you wanted and does not work like it. All 45 of them swap to the real item the moment the discovery lands.

The crude items keep the vanilla display name, so nothing in your inventory announces that it is the degraded version. Working that out is the point.
